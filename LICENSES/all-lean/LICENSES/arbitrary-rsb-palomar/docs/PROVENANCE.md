## Mathematical scope retained from the development

[Coverage](../COVERAGE.md) gives the current-paper mapping. The following scope qualifications apply:

- The original `DepthPaper` transition restricts degrees to at least four and uses an internal positive atomic Hessian invariant. The revised `GeneralDepthPaper` wrappers allow quadratic terms under ξ″(0)<1 and derive that invariant from the paper’s weaker nondegeneracy conditions. `atomic_hessian` requires chart criticality and negative contact curvature; it does not assume a minimizer or Hessian positivity.
- The revised universal theorem is for every supplied admissible paper-nondegenerate seed. The old all-depth existential transition remains a distinct historical statement. The revised finite-covariance proof uses actual normalized mixture/clock correspondences, rather than assuming a transition or identifying an optimizer by node count.
- Arbitrary-measure time PDE statements use a distributional interpretation. Closed overlap endpoints and atomic cell boundaries retain within-interval or one-sided derivatives.
- The retained finite-volume entropy-positivity argument remains a dependency. New proofs establish the exact atomic temperature-derivative identity and equality of diffusion entropy with physical pressure entropy in the section’s standing nondegenerate/subcritical context.
- Revised asymptotic bridges identify the inverse-curve coefficient with the paper’s derivative ratio, use the actual scalar-objective mass derivative, provide the full δ≤η/2 window and logarithmic endpoint estimate, and prove the diagonal small-o law. Appendix C.1 preserves the exact V^ℓ budget and prescribed decay rate.
- Intermediate standing-data results and atomic charts retain explicit local hypotheses. Universal revised wrappers construct their witnesses. Prop-valued targets are specifications; corresponding theorems supply proofs. The sharp Jagannath–Tobasco gradient estimate preserves the cited constant and attribution.
- Lemma 2.2's full finite-support stochastic clause now uses an independently constructed physical-time standard Brownian motion on the original probability space. The same driver supplies the actual state's square-root-volatility Wiener integral and the actual Hessian's physical-time integral. Both endpoint atoms and zero origin volatility are included. The alternate proof uses normalized-time Itô calculus, inverse-clock Wiener transport, and L² convergence of literal square-root-volatility-times-Hessian left sums. Their nested limits are defined independently of the gradient value. Equality holds almost surely at each fixed time, and the actual continuous gradient is proved to be a continuous modification. Adaptation modulo null sets and future-increment independence are proved in the original clock past; raw natural-filtration equality and an arbitrary-measure Hessian-integral extension are not asserted.

The paper does not fix the norm for these coordinate neighborhoods; equivalence of finite-dimensional norms changes local constants. The four numbered remarks remain context with related proved APIs, rather than additional exact numbered theorem claims. Remark A.8 has supporting all-positive mixed bounds, but the Banach smoothness API is proved on mass (0,2); its entire all-positive wording is not separately certified.

The Appendix C.2 extension follows an alternative proof route with the same even monotone tilt as the paper. It compares bounded conditional-sign expectations using explicit Gaussian reflection and independent-copy association, then identifies the actual Cole–Hopf derivative ratio. Nonempty compact I is explicit in Appendix C.1 because its stated decay constant uses min I. Lemma A.1(iii) uses a bounded backward Gaussian Fourier argument in place of the printed Girsanov proof. Lemma A.7 proves scaling for independently defined solutions driven by the same original-clock noise, using pathwise uniqueness.
